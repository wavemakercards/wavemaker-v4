Write with Wavemaker

Use the Writer to draft a book, split it into sections, and export a copy when you are ready.

Looking for more help? Visit the Wavemaker Cards Reddit community to join discussions with other users, or watch the Wavemaker video tutorials for step-by-step guidance.

These are community resources. Thank you to the Reddit contributors and @authorkimmymoon for sharing their time and knowledge.

1. Start a manuscript

  1. Open a project in Wavemaker Cards. If you are starting fresh, choose Get Started to create one.
  2. Open the navigation menu and choose Writer (the book icon).
  3. On the Writer screen, choose the + button to create a manuscript. It opens a new, untitled manuscript.
  4. Give it a title and, if useful, a short description.

2. Organize chapters and sections

The panel on the left holds your manuscript structure. Use the folder button at the bottom to add a folder, and the page button to add a writing section. Select a folder or section first to add items inside it or nearby.

Choose an item in the left panel to open it. You can rename a section from its title field above the editor, or rename a folder from its title field when the folder is open. Drag items in the panel to reorder them.

3. Write and format

Click in the editor and start writing. The formatting toolbar lets you use bold, italic, strikethrough, headings, lists, and images. Hover over a toolbar button to see its label.

Wavemaker saves edits as you make them. The Writer shows word counts for a section and for the whole manuscript.

Tip: Use folders to group chapters or parts, and writing sections for the text you want to edit and count separately.

4. Export your manuscript

Return to the manuscript overview by choosing the manuscript name at the top of the left panel. Choose Export a .doc file of this manuscript to download the manuscript as a Word-compatible document. To export only a folder, open that folder and choose its export button.

5. Save a project backup

Word export gives you a readable document, but it is not a full project backup. To back up your Wavemaker project, use the Save button in the top toolbar and keep the downloaded .wm4 file somewhere safe. You can also connect Google Drive from the toolbar to store or sync project data.

Keep a backup: Your writing is stored in the browser for the current project. Download a project backup regularly, especially before clearing browser data or moving to another device.
